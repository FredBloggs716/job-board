# 6-Week Charlie Francis Sprint Training Programme

## Overview

This programme is based on Charlie Francis's High-Low training model. The central principle is that the Central Nervous System (CNS) must be protected: high-intensity work and low-intensity work are never mixed on the same day, and adequate recovery is scheduled between high-intensity sessions.

**Training Intensity Zones**
- **High Intensity (HI):** >95% max speed — speed work, acceleration, power lifting
- **Low Intensity (LI):** 60–75% max speed — tempo runs, bodyweight circuits, mobility

**Weekly Structure**
- Mon / Wed / Fri: High Intensity
- Tue / Thu / Sat: Low Intensity
- Sun: Full rest

**Abbreviations**
| Abbrev | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| A | Acceleration runs |
| S | Speed (fly) runs |
| SE | Speed endurance |
| T | Tempo |
| SP | Sprint Plyo |
| Wt | Weight room |

## Phase 1 — Acceleration Development (Weeks 1–2)

Focus is on short acceleration mechanics, ground contact quality, and building work capacity through tempo.

---

### Week 1

#### Monday — HI: Acceleration + Lower Weights
**Warm-Up** (25–30 min)
- 2 × 800 m jog
- A-skip, B-skip, high knees, butt kicks — 2 × 30 m each
- Wickets at walking pace × 2
- 3 progressive 60 m strides at 80 / 85 / 90%

**Speed Work**
- 3 × 10 m block start (full recovery — 3 min)
- 3 × 20 m block start (full recovery — 4 min)
- 3 × 30 m block start (full recovery — 5 min)
- Walk back between all reps

**Weights — Lower**
- Back Squat: 4 × 4 @ 75–80% 1RM
- Romanian Deadlift: 3 × 6 @ 70%
- Leg Press: 3 × 8
- Leg Curl: 3 × 8
- Calf Raise: 3 × 12

---

#### Tuesday — LI: Tempo + Core
**Warm-Up** (15 min)
- 2 × 200 m jog, drills

**Tempo Volume: 2,000 m total at 65–70% max**
- 10 × 100 m on 60 s rest
- Walk 100 m between sets; rest 3 min after 5 reps

**Core Circuit** (3 rounds)
- Plank: 40 s
- Side plank (each side): 30 s
- Lying leg raise: 15
- Back extension: 15

---

#### Wednesday — HI: Acceleration + Upper Weights
**Warm-Up** (25–30 min)
- Same as Monday

**Speed Work**
- 4 × 20 m block start (5 min recovery)
- 3 × 30 m block start (5–6 min recovery)
- 2 × 40 m block start (6 min recovery)

**Weights — Upper**
- Bench Press: 4 × 5 @ 75%
- Chin-Up (weighted if possible): 4 × 5
- Military Press: 3 × 6
- Barbell Row: 3 × 6
- Tricep Pushdown: 3 × 10

---

#### Thursday — LI: Tempo + Mobility
**Tempo Volume: 1,800 m**
- 3 × 200 m at 65% (90 s rest)
- 6 × 100 m at 70% (60 s rest)

**Mobility (20 min)**
- Hip flexor stretches, piriformis, hamstring, thoracic rotation
- 2 × 10 hip circles, leg swings (front/back, lateral)

---

#### Friday — HI: Acceleration / Special Endurance intro + Full Body Weights
**Warm-Up** (25–30 min)

**Speed Work**
- 3 × 30 m (6 min recovery)
- 2 × 40 m (7 min recovery)
- 2 × 50 m (8 min recovery)

**Weights — Full Body**
- Power Clean: 4 × 3 @ 70%
- Front Squat: 3 × 5 @ 70%
- Glute-Ham Raise: 3 × 8
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise: 3 × 12
- Farmer's Carry: 3 × 30 m

---

#### Saturday — LI: Tempo
**Tempo Volume: 2,000 m**
- 2 × 300 m at 65% (2 min rest)
- 4 × 200 m at 68% (90 s rest)
- 4 × 100 m at 70% (60 s rest)

---

#### Sunday — Rest

## Phase 2 — Speed Development (Weeks 3–4)

Acceleration distances increase. Fly runs introduced. Lifting volume reduces slightly; intensity increases.

---

**Warm-Up** (30 min)
- Extended drills, 4 progressive strides up to 95%

**Speed Work**
- 3 × 30 m block (6 min)
- 3 × 30 m fly (20 m build-up + 30 m timed zone) — 8 min recovery
- 2 × 40 m block (7 min)

**Weights — Lower**

- Romanian Deadlift: 3 × 5 @ 75%
- Leg Curl: 3 × 6
- Calf Raise: 3 × 10
- Reverse Hyper: 3 × 12

## Phase 3 — Speed Endurance & Competition Prep (Weeks 5–6)

Lifting volume reduces further. Sprint volumes stay controlled but intensity rises to near-maximal. Speed endurance work targets race-specific distances.

---

### Week 5

#### Monday — HI: Max Velocity + Lower Weights
**Speed Work**
- 3 × 30 m fly (8 min — max effort)
- 2 × 60 m (95–98%) — 12 min
- 1 × 80 m (95%) — 15 min

**Weights — Lower (reduced volume)**
- Back Squat: 4 × 2 @ 88–92%
- Romanian Deadlift: 3 × 4 @ 78%
- Leg Curl: 3 × 6
- Calf Raise: 2 × 10

---

#### Tuesday — LI: Tempo + Core
**Tempo Volume: 1,800 m (reduced)**
- 3 × 200 m at 68%
- 6 × 100 m at 72%

**Core** (2 rounds)
- Hanging leg raise: 12
- Pallof press: 12
- Plank: 45 s
- Side plank: 35 s each

---

#### Wednesday — HI: Speed Endurance + Upper Weights
**Speed Work**
- 1 × 80 m (95%) — 15 min
- 1 × 100 m (93%) — 18 min
- 1 × 120 m (90%) — 20 min

**Weights — Upper (reduced volume)**
- Bench Press: 4 × 2 @ 87%
- Weighted Chin-Up: 3 × 4
- Military Press: 3 × 4 @ 78%
- Barbell Row: 3 × 4

---

#### Thursday — LI: Tempo + Mobility
**Tempo Volume: 1,600 m**
- 2 × 300 m at 65%
- 5 × 100 m at 70%

**Extensive Mobility (25 min)**

---

#### Friday — HI: Competition Simulation + Full Body Weights (light)
**Speed Work**
- 2 × 30 m block (6 min)
- 1 × 60 m block (max) — 15 min
- 1 × 100 m (95%) — 20 min rest

**Weights — Full Body (light)**
- Power Clean: 3 × 2 @ 80%
- Front Squat: 3 × 3 @ 75%
- Glute-Ham Raise: 2 × 6
- Sled Sprint: 2 × 20 m (light load, fast)

---

#### Saturday — LI: Tempo (short)
**Tempo Volume: 1,400 m**
- 7 × 200 m at 65–68%

---

#### Sunday — Rest

---

### Week 6 — Competition Week

Volumes drop sharply. Quality and freshness of the CNS are paramount. All sessions are short. Taper for race day (assumed Friday/Saturday of this week).

---

#### Monday — HI: Sharpening
**Speed Work**
- 3 × 20 m block (5 min) — max effort
- 2 × 30 m fly (8 min) — max effort
- 2 × 40 m block (8 min)

**Weights — Lower (minimal)**
- Back Squat: 3 × 2 @ 85%
- Leg Curl: 2 × 6

---

#### Tuesday — LI: Short Tempo
**Tempo Volume: 1,200 m**
- 6 × 100 m at 70%
- 3 × 100 m at 73%

**Mobility (20 min)**

---

#### Wednesday — HI: Final Sharpener (light)
**Speed Work**
- 2 × 20 m block (5 min)
- 2 × 30 m fly (8 min — controlled 95%)
- 1 × 60 m (90%) — 15 min

**No weights**

---

#### Thursday — LI / Active Recovery
- 20 min easy jog
- 15 min full mobility routine
- Ice bath or contrast shower recommended

---

#### Friday — Competition Day
- Warm-up protocol: 45 min
  - 2 × 800 m easy jog
  - Full drill sequence (A-skips, B-skips, high knees, ankling)
  - 4 × 30 m progressive strides (80 / 85 / 90 / 95%)
  - 2 × block starts (30 m — near max)
  - 10 min static and dynamic stretching
- **Race**

---

#### Saturday — LI or Rest
**If competing Saturday instead of Friday:**
- Same competition warm-up protocol as above

**If Friday race only:**
- Full rest or 15 min jog + mobility

---

#### Sunday — Rest / Recovery
- Light walk
- Massage or foam rolling recommended

## Key Principles Summary

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **CNS management** | Never combine HI and LI on the same day |
| **Full recovery between HI reps** | Walk-back rest; never cut short |
| **Speed work done fresh** | Speed before weights; never reversed |
| **Tempo as active recovery** | 65–75% max only — aerobic without CNS cost |
| **Progressive overload** | Distances and loads increase week-on-week; Week 6 tapers sharply |
| **Quality over quantity** | Fewer reps at true max effort beats many reps at sub-max |

## Nutrition & Recovery Notes

- **Pre-HI session:** High-carbohydrate meal 2–3 hours before
- **Post-HI session:** Protein + carbohydrate within 30 minutes
- **Sleep:** 8–10 hours; the CNS recovers primarily during sleep
- **CNS fatigue signs:** Drop in power output, coordination errors, lack of aggression — reduce load immediately
- **Hydration:** 2–3 litres per day minimum; more on HI days
